Hunt's Duke of Gloucester

A Distinguished Gloucestershire Apple

Hunt's Duke of Gloucester is a fine dessert apple with strong connections to Gloucestershire. Named for the Duke of Gloucester, this variety exemplifies the quality of traditional English apples and the county's fruit growing heritage.

Identification

  • Size: Medium to large
  • Shape: Round-conical, regular and attractive
  • Skin: Golden yellow with red flush and streaks
  • Flavor: Rich, sweet, aromatic with excellent balance

Season and Use

Picking Time: Late September to October

Storage: Excellent keeper, stores well into winter

Use: Dessert apple of the highest quality

As a variety bearing the county's name, Hunt's Duke of Gloucester holds special place in Gloucestershire's pomological heritage and our conservation efforts.
