Gloucester Royal

Royalty Among Gloucestershire Apples

Gloucester Royal is a historic apple variety with strong connections to the city and county of Gloucester. This attractive dessert apple carries the county's name with pride and represents an important part of local pomological heritage.

Identification

  • Size: Medium to large
  • Shape: Round to round-conical, regular shape
  • Skin: Yellow-green background with attractive red flush and stripes
  • Flavor: Sweet with good aromatic quality, well-balanced

Season and Use

Picking Time: September to October

Storage: Good keeping quality, stores for several months

Use: Primarily dessert, excellent for fresh eating

As one of the county's name-bearing varieties, Gloucester Royal holds special significance in our work to preserve Gloucestershire's orchard heritage.
