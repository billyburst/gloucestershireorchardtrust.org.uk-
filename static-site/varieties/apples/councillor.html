Councillor

A Local Heritage Variety

Councillor is a traditional apple variety associated with Gloucestershire. This dual-purpose apple has been valued in local orchards for both eating and cooking qualities.

Identification

  • Size: Medium to large
  • Shape: Round-conical
  • Skin: Green-yellow background with red flush
  • Flavor: Good balance of sweet and sharp when fresh, becomes sweeter with storage

Season and Use

Picking Time: October

Storage: Keeps well through winter

Use: Dual-purpose - suitable for both eating fresh and cooking
