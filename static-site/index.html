25 Free Spins No Deposit Bonuses Limits and Caps

Two of the most important limits to be aware of with 25 free spins no deposit bonuses are wagering requirements and maximum win caps.

Wagering requirements restrict how much you need to bet before you can withdraw bonus winnings. They are usually expressed as a multiple of the bonus amount, like 10x, 20x, or 30x. Higher wagering makes it harder to actually cash out winnings. For example, if you win £20 from your free spins with a 30x wagering requirement, you would need to place £600 worth of bets before you can withdraw your winnings.

Maximum win limits restrict how much real money you can withdraw from no deposit free spins, regardless of how much you actually win. For example, if you win £500 from free spins with a £100 max cashout, you'll only be able to withdraw £100. This is an important factor to consider when evaluating the true value of a free spins offer.
