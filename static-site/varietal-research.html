Varietal Research

Preserving Gloucestershire's Fruit Heritage

The Gloucestershire Orchard Trust is committed to identifying, documenting, and preserving the county's unique heritage fruit varieties. Our varietal research program is essential to understanding and conserving Gloucestershire's pomological heritage.

Our Research Focus

We conduct extensive research into three main categories of fruit:

  • Apples - Including dessert, culinary, and cider varieties native to or historically grown in Gloucestershire
  • Perry Pears - The traditional pears used to make perry, Gloucestershire's signature drink
  • Plums - Heritage plum varieties that have been cultivated in the county for generations

Research Methods

Our varietal research involves:

  • Orchard surveys and fruit identification
  • Historical documentation and archive research
  • DNA analysis and pomological description
  • Collaboration with national fruit collections
  • Recording oral histories from local growers
